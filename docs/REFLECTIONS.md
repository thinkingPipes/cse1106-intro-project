Expanding the Original Project

Rather than limiting the project to the basic example provided in the assignment, I decided to experiment with expanding its functionality.

The original requirements focus primarily on memory and storage utilization. I wanted to take that idea a little further by incorporating additional information about the Raspberry Pi's operating condition, particularly CPU utilization and temperature monitoring.

CPU utilization can help indicate when a device is under a heavy processing load, while temperature monitoring provides additional information about the physical operating conditions of the hardware. These measurements are especially relevant for small computers like the Raspberry Pi, where cooling and available resources can sometimes be limited.

Another feature I wanted to incorporate was a visual dashboard. Instead of relying entirely on printed text or individual status messages, I wanted to explore how the diagnostic information could be presented in a more organized and readable format.

The goal is to make it easier for someone using the application to understand the current condition of the device without having to interpret several separate measurements manually. Ideally, a user should be able to look at the dashboard and quickly determine whether the Raspberry Pi is operating normally or whether something requires attention.

I also intend to preserve the original requirements of the assignment, including configurable warning thresholds, diagnostic logging, and the LED warning indicator. The additional features are meant to build upon the original project rather than replace its required functionality.
Use of Artificial Intelligence (Codex in this case)

Another important part of my approach involves the use of artificial intelligence during development.

The course syllabus specifically states that AI tools are permitted as long as students are honest and transparent about their use. With that in mind, I decided to incorporate AI assistance into my development process while openly acknowledging its involvement.

My goal was not simply to have an AI tool reproduce the example assignment. Instead, I wanted to explore whether AI-assisted development could help me take a relatively straightforward introductory project and expand it into something with additional practical functionality.

AI has been useful as a tool for exploring programming approaches, discussing possible features, understanding technical concepts, and working through some of the challenges involved in developing a more complicated application.

I think this is particularly interesting in the context of an introductory Computer Science course. As someone still developing my programming knowledge, I can use AI assistance to explore ideas that might otherwise be difficult to implement at my current experience level.

At the same time, I recognize that generating code and understanding code are two different things. Part of my objective is to examine how the application works, understand the purpose of its different components, and become more familiar with the programming concepts involved.

I believe that learning how to use these tools responsibly, while maintaining an understanding of the underlying technology, is becoming an increasingly useful skill in computer science and information technology.
